Meet Slimsheddy

At Slimsheddy Makeup Artistry, beauty is more than makeup — it is confidence, elegance, and transformation. We specialize in flawless glam for brides, party guests, birthdays, photoshoots, and special occasions. Every face is a canvas, and every look is crafted with precision, passion, and premium products that last all day.

Based in Abeokuta, Ogun State, and available to travel across Nigeria, we bring luxury beauty to your doorstep.
