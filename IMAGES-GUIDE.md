## Tips

- Always use JPG format for photos (smaller file size, faster loading)
- Compress images using TinyPNG (tinypng.com) before uploading
- Keep portrait photos at 800×1000px or similar ratios
- The gallery images in /images/gallery/ are also used as video thumbnails
  so they should be high quality
- All filenames are CASE SENSITIVE — use lowercase only
